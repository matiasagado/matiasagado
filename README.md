I'm [**Matias**](https://matiasagado.com), a software engineer in San Francisco. I like going from problem to production: sitting with the people who have the problem, figuring out what they actually need, and shipping it.

- I'm an **AI Model Evaluation Fellow** at [**Handshake AI**](https://joinhandshake.com), where I work with AI researchers to test LLM outputs and give structured feedback that makes model responses more accurate and useful.
- I'm building **Foothold**, a multi-tenant platform that helps college students find their clubs, events and people.
- I built **ShiftGrid** for Santa Fe Post Acute's dietary team, replacing paper schedules and saving their manager about 10 hours a week.

Before engineering I worked in revenue-driven, client-facing roles, from cold-calling distressed property owners to running high-volume service. That's where I learned to understand people before building for them.

Outside of work I follow what's happening in AI and systems, and I travel and backpack whenever I get the chance.
